About me

"I don't want to just copy code. I want to understand every line I write."

I'm Rehman Ali, a college student doing a CIT diploma at the Christian Technical Training Center (CTTC). Every day I work toward one goal: becoming an AI-augmented full-stack developer.

I started with HTML5, CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript, and I practiced them by building real websites and publishing them on Netlify. Country Explorer and ProVision taught me how to work with data, search, filters, and sorting in plain JavaScript.

At college I'm learning web development, graphic designing, and databases, and on my own I'm getting comfortable with Git, GitHub, and SQL. Next I'm learning React, Node.js, and SQL to expand into backend development.

I like simple, clean design that feels good to use, and I learn best by building something and fixing it until it works.
